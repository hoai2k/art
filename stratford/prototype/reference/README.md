# Stratford Squirrel: reference images

Reference photographs and drawings for modelling the game (the eastern grey squirrel, downtown Stratford, Ontario, and ambient swans and geese). **Never published**: this folder is excluded from the site build; it is committed to Git only so that modellers can look at it. Keep it lean (images are at most 1280 px on the long side, JPEG, metadata stripped).

Sources are openly licensed or public-domain only: Wikimedia Commons (CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain, and Ontario's Open Government Licence for the Archives of Ontario photos) and iNaturalist observations whose *photo* licence is CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA. No non-commercial or all-rights-reserved images are included. Attribution is in the tables below (author, licence, source page). Collected 2026-11-01; every image was looked at and mislabelled or poor ones were dropped.

Notes for users of the set:
- Where a file shows a related species (red squirrel or fox squirrel skeletons, because no grey-squirrel skeleton plate was found) the description says so.
- Many Stratford photos are by Chris Woodrich (2025-08-04, summer, green trees); the Lake Victoria photos from that day show the lake drained. Autumn views are few (`avon_river...`, `lake_victoria_autumn_willow_water`, house and street shots with early autumn colour).
- Colour photos of the Avon bridges from the Archives of Ontario are marked OGL-ON.
- Subfolders `sheets/` and `data/` belong to other agents and are not described here.

## squirrel/ (65 images)

Grey and black morph; head/face, full-body side, climbing, head-first descent, leaping, gallop, swimming, tail, skulls and skeleton diagrams. Not found openly licensed: Muybridge-style or burst-sequence locomotion plates, clear hind-foot (reversed ankle) close-ups, and a grey-squirrel skeleton diagram (the skeletons here are fox squirrel and red squirrel).
