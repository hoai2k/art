Instructions

You are an eastern grey squirrel in downtown Stratford, Ontario, in mid-October. Run, leap and climb through Market Square, along the Avon River and around the theatres. There is nothing to lose and no clock.

What to do

  • Find the landmarks. Run up to a famous place for the first time and it is written into your journal.
  • Gather nuts. Acorns under oaks, walnuts and conkers under other trees, and a few hidden on roofs, up trees and in planters. Run over one to pick it up.
  • Bury and dig. Press Dig to bury a nut you carry where you stand. Press it again on the spot to dig it up. Buried nuts are kept.
  • Climb. Brick, stone and wooden walls, tree trunks, branches and fences can be gripped. Glass, metal and stucco cannot. Swimming is possible, but squirrels do not love it.
